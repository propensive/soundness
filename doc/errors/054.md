# `SN-054` — `Discovery.Error`

Raised when DNS-SD service discovery (`syndesis`) cannot advertise, browse or resolve, or when
a service, instance or TXT value is not valid.

## Cause

`Discovery.Service`, `Discovery.Instance` and `Discovery.Txt` validate their parts on
construction; an advertisement probes for its name and gives up after too many conflicts; a
resolution waits for the instance's records within a timeout; and a backend must be able to
start. The message is `service discovery failed because $reason`.

## Variants

### `SN-054.1` — `Conflict`

Another host kept claiming every name tried for an advertisement, through the renaming limit.

**Example.** `[↯SN-054.1] service discovery failed because the name Gondor._fury._tcp.local could not be claimed after repeated conflicts`

**Resolution.** Choose a distinct instance name, or stop the other advertiser.

### `SN-054.2` — `Timeout`

An instance's SRV, TXT or address records did not arrive within the resolution's timeout.

**Example.** `[↯SN-054.2] service discovery failed because the instance Gondor._fury._tcp.local did not resolve before the timeout`

**Resolution.** The instance may have left the network; browse again, or lengthen the timeout.

### `SN-054.3` — `InvalidName`

An instance label is empty, or a label or name exceeds the DNS limits (63 octets to a label,
255 to a name).

**Example.** `[↯SN-054.3] service discovery failed because the name  is not a valid DNS-SD name`

**Resolution.** Shorten the label; it may contain any UTF-8, including dots and spaces.

### `SN-054.4` — `InvalidTxt`

A TXT key is empty, not printable ASCII, or contains `=`; or a `key=value` pair exceeds 255
octets.

**Example.** `[↯SN-054.4] service discovery failed because the TXT entry a=b is not valid`

**Resolution.** Keys are short printable ASCII names; put the `=` in the value, and split long
values across keys.

### `SN-054.5` — `InvalidService`

A service name is empty, longer than fifteen characters, or begins with the underscore that the
DNS form adds.

**Example.** `[↯SN-054.5] service discovery failed because the service name _fury is not valid`

**Resolution.** Give the registered service name without its underscore: `fury`, not `_fury`.

### `SN-054.6` — `Unavailable`

The backend could not start: the socket responder found no multicast-capable interface, or
could not join the mDNS group.

**Example.** `[↯SN-054.6] service discovery failed because no service discovery backend could be started`

**Resolution.** Check the host's network interfaces, and that port 5353 can be bound with reuse.
